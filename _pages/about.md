I am a graduate Electrical Engineering student at University of Pennsylvania, with focus in designing hardware accelerators, ASIC and hardware-software Co-development. My work focuses on designing and implementing hardware systems, exploring digital architectures, and building practical engineering solutions.

Before Penn, I was the R&D engineer at KMT, where I designed an Inductive proximity sensor end to end from bench-top setup to finished sensor unit. Additionally, as a research intern at Photonic Sensors Lab, IIT Gandhinagar, I built a laser-driver protection circuit and helped integrate a UAV-mounted water-vapor sensing payload, which led to co-authorship on two papers. I also design FPGA accelerators in System Verilog, including a systolic-array matrix multiplier, a Smith-Waterman aligner, and a CNN for ECG classification.

## Research Interests

My interests include FPGA-based hardware accelerators, and memory centric and low latency computing units. I am particularly interested in understanding how hardware design decisions influence performance, resource utilization, and system efficiency.

## Selected Projects

I work on digital hardware and embedded engineering projects, including processor architectures, FPGA implementations, and peripheral interfacing.

Some areas I explore include:

- **Digital Design and FPGA:** RTL development, processor pipelines, hazard handling, and hardware verification.
- **Embedded Systems:** Microcontroller programming, peripheral interfacing, and hardware-software integration.
- **Hardware Architecture:** Exploring datapaths, memory access, data movement, and efficient computation.

Visit the Projects section to explore individual implementations, source code, technical documentation, and demonstration videos.
